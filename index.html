WINDOWS APP

Calendar Veil

A small Windows tray app for a household PC. Ten minutes before each event on your Google Calendar, it floats a see-through reminder over whatever you're doing, and for Zoom or Google Meet events it can join the call for you with camera and microphone off.

What it does

WhenWhat you see
10 minutes before a timed eventA translucent "veil" with the time and title. Clicks pass through it until you click it.
Event with a Zoom or Meet linkA Join button that enters the meeting with camera and mic off.
First use of the PC each morningOne veil listing today's all-day events.
Any timeA tray icon showing your next event and today's list.

Google access

Calendar Veil asks Google for one permission: read-only access to your calendars (calendar.readonly). It cannot create, change or delete events, and it does not read email, contacts or anything else. Each person signs in from their own Windows account, and nobody else on the PC can see their calendar.

How your data is handled is described in the privacy policy.
