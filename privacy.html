CALENDAR VEIL

Privacy policy

Calendar Veil reads your Google Calendar so it can remind you of events on your own Windows PC. Your calendar data stays on that PC and is never sent to anyone except Google.

What it reads

With your permission (the Google scope calendar.readonly), the app reads the list of your calendars and the events from now until the end of tomorrow: titles, times, locations, descriptions, meeting links and your own response (accepted, declined). It does not read any other Google data.

What it stores, and where

ItemWhereWhy
Google sign-in tokenYour Windows account's local app folder, encrypted with Windows (DPAPI) so only your Windows account can read itSo you don't have to sign in every time
A copy of upcoming eventsSame local folderSo reminders still work if the internet drops
Which reminders were already shownSame local folderSo the same reminder isn't shown twice
A log file (includes event titles)Same local folderTo troubleshoot problems on that PC

Nothing is uploaded to the developer or any other server. The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking. It talks only to Google's Calendar API, and, when you press Join, opens the meeting in Zoom or your browser.

How the data is used

Only to show reminders and the tray list on your PC, and to open meeting links when you ask. Calendar Veil's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • Sign out from the tray menu deletes your token and the copy of your events from the PC.
  • You can also revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for Calendar Veil. Last updated 23 October 2026.
